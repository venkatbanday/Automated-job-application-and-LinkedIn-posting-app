    4. Should you approach recruiters?

    Yes, but be selective. A few targeted messages to the right people beat hundreds of applications. Mass applying through job boards rarely works for experienced candidates: most replies come from referrals, direct contact with hiring teams and specialist recruiters.

    Worth your time

    • Specialist recruiters in your exact field and city, who already place your type of role.
    • In-house talent teams and hiring managers at companies you picked.
    • Interim and contract recruiters, if you can start quickly.
    • Former colleagues and people who share your background: ask for advice first, a job second.
    • A short, specific message with one real result and a clear ask.

    Waste of time or risky

    • Sending the same CV to many general agencies.
    • Anyone asking you to pay to be placed. Legitimate UK agencies are paid by employers, not by you.
    • “Job guarantee” courses: read the refund and interview conditions before you pay.
    • Recruiters who will not name the employer, or who ask for bank or ID details early.
    • Applying to roles far below your level with a CV that hides your experience.

    A simple weekly plan

    • 10 targeted messages (recruiters or hiring managers) and 5 tailored applications.
    • 2 conversations with people in your field.
    • Update your tracker every Friday and drop channels that give no replies after 4 weeks.
