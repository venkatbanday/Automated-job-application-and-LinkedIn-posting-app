3. Storage and privacy

  • Career Hub uses the browser key ch.
  • LinkedIn profile fields use keys beginning lp_.
  • Other tools may store saved reports or practice records under CareerOS-specific keys.
  • Browser storage can be cleared by browser settings, private-mode closure or device cleanup. Export backups regularly.
  • Do not use a shared/public computer for personal career data.
  • AI keys entered into the new AI pages are held in page memory only; close the tab to discard them.
Restore warning

A restore can overwrite local records. Export a fresh backup first.
